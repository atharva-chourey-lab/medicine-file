## 1. Structural finding and method

| Claim in paper | Value / wording | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Every traditional date tested against foreign dated records: Chinese catalogues, pilgrims and annals; Arabic bibliographies and compendia; Tibetan translation colophons; dated physical manuscripts | MED header |
| Verdict classes | CORRECTED / CORROBORATED / BOUNDED / NO BITE | MED header |
| Foreign-record rule | A foreign record dates the event it directly records — a translation, a catalogue entry, a sojourn — and constrains the Indian original only as a bound, never as a composition date | MED header |
| Structural finding | The great saṃhitās travelled west, not east: Caraka and Suśruta translated into Arabic in Baghdad and quoted there by 850; no Chinese bibliography records a translation of either; the Tibetan canon took only Vāgbhaṭa's epitome | MED header (structural finding) |
| What reached China | Ayurveda as practice and formulary — Jīvaka-attributed prescriptions, an Indian eye treatise, a massage method, court physicians — not the books themselves | MED header |
| Split in the anchors | The canon's hard foreign anchors are Arabic; China's anchors date the system's presence and prestige; Yijing (691) is the sharpest bracket on Vāgbhaṭa | MED header |

## 5. Gaps and handling notes

1. **Single-source paper.** Every claim in `paper.md` derives from MED alone. Authorities named inside MED — Meulenbeld, Meyerhof, Lévi, Takakusu, Deshpande, Sander, Callewaert, Hoernle, Fan — are cited in the paper only as MED names them; no bibliographic detail beyond MED's has been added.
2. **Sui titles.** MED names seven of the eleven Sui-catalogue Indian medical titles and gives the total as eleven "by the usual count". The paper reproduces the seven named titles and the total exactly in that form; it does not supply the remaining four.
3. **Grades preserved.** Where MED gives a split or qualified grade — Caraka BOUNDED for the tradition and CORROBORATED for existence and present form; the Nāgārjuna identification NO BITE against the Suśruta text BOUNDED; Yijing's epitomist identification probable, not firm; the Bower anchor probable, not firm, because palaeographic — the paper carries the qualification in the same place.
4. **No CORRECTED verdict** appears in MED's table for this dossier, and none is claimed in the paper.
